One unicorn. One horn. One stolen move. Nothing else is fixed.

Random Duel Rainbow is a keyboard-controlled boss-rush action game where almost everything is generated at random. Three guardians have stolen the colors of the rainbow, and each one is built fresh for your run: its attack shapes, timing, combos, reach and defenses are all rolled from a seed, so you never fight the same guardian twice. Read the windup, parry with your horn, and punish the opening.

Every guardian you defeat lets you copy ONE of its moves into your horn. A wide sweep to make space, a quick thrust to slip into a short gap, a heavy slam to break posture, a charge, a shot, or a rain of falling strikes — your single stolen move changes how you fight the next foe. Try a move on a practice target before you commit.

The music is random too. Each run generates its own melody from the seed, adding bass, lead and echo layers as you climb.

Restore the rainbow by defeating all three, then continue into Prism Ascent, an endless climb where enemies grow tougher and a mutated boss appears every third victory. Your best clear count is saved.

Random, but fair and shareable: generation keeps every guardian within safe difficulty limits, and the same seed always reproduces the same opponents and the same music. Share a seed and challenge a friend to the exact same duel, or press N for a brand-new one.

CONTROLS
Move: A/D or Left/Right
Jump: W, Up or Space
Attack: J or Z
Parry: K or X (watch for the white outline on an incoming attack)
Roll: L or C
Pause: Escape

After a victory: choose a move with A/D, press T to try it, R to go back, Enter to confirm.

Desktop browser with keyboard required. Audio starts after your first key press.
